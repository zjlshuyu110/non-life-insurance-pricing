What was controlled?

406,807 policies for inner fitting; 101,702 for validation. Binning, categories and scaling were learned on the permitted training split. A 75-feature MLP with ReLU hidden layers and a positive log link minimized exposure-weighted Tweedie loss (p=1.9). Validation selected the architecture and epoch, then each seed was refitted on all 508,509 outer training policies.

The reference GLMs retain their original settings. The additional tuned Tweedie uses alpha 1.0, selected from a four-value validation grid. MLP seed 0 was predeclared as primary; no seed was selected by test performance and no post-hoc calibration was applied.

Where the conclusion stops

This is one random holdout, a limited model search and a EUR 200k capped target. Calibration, ranking and deviance measure different properties. Bootstrap intervals assume independent policies, condition on fitted models and omit training uncertainty. Future-year results may differ. Further tuning needs fresh evaluation data.
